Jove Belane

“I kept the lights on.”

‹ All Characters

Profile

Seventy-seven years on an island that studied what he brought with him and built the dark out of it. The things in the treeline wore faces he knew from locker rooms. The island did not invent a single one of them.

He did not spend that time surviving alone. He built a town, and the town held, because Jove decided early that a place with people in it was worth more than a place he could defend by himself. Championship belts nailed above every door. Not sentiment — they worked, and once something works you stop asking why.

The omni serum kept him standing far past the point a body should have quit. Seventy-seven years of cellular rewriting leaves a margin nobody has measured. He takes the last dose at Holliday Sinclair's door, and what it does is not what anyone in the stairwell expected.

The intelligence is in the economy. He says four words and you understand you were asking the wrong question.

On Joining

He is not on the quest for anyone's redemption, least of all his own. The fastest route to Brennan Devlin runs through this plan, and that is the entire reason.

Asked directly, he would have used the championship for himself. He does not pretend otherwise, which puts him ahead of most of the room.
