Amy Stake

“I'm just correct.”

‹ All Characters

Profile

A trad wife influencer who used to be a wrestler, sealed for seventy-seven years inside a house that answered every domestic ambition she ever performed and never once gave her anything to beat.

The house expanded the way a wound expands — infinitely, and with organisation. Every room had a purpose. The pantry was a masterwork. It was also the single cruellest object in the film, because a masterwork with no one to serve it to is just an inventory.

She was serving a final course to dimensional constructs when The Mop arrived, because leaving guests mid-meal would have been rude, and Amy's manners survived the dimension intact. “Are you here to take me somewhere I can hit something?” “Yes.” “One moment.”

Clever, snide, magnetic, and correct often enough that the snideness has never cost her anything.

Adjudication

It is Amy who rules on whether the bag of utopia championships counts. She rules that it does. Hatchet and A$hley have never once questioned the ruling, which is the only thing they have never questioned.

In the penthouse she gets more offence in on Devlin than JD or Jove managed. Devlin respects it. That is not the same as it working.
