Profile

A wrestling critic who has never wrestled and considers this a credential rather than a gap. He hosts On the Matter of Wrestling, where he explains what the people actually doing it got wrong.

He is right more often than anyone in the locker room is comfortable with, which is the reason he is still booked and the reason nobody sits with him.
