The Mop

“The Mop got them back.”

‹ All Characters

Profile

An industrial mop, frayed at the edges with the specific dignity of a thing that has been used hard and survived it. Extremely accomplished. The running problem, across every match it has ever had, is that nobody knows how to fight a mop.

It was headed to WrestleMecca in 2026 and did not arrive in the way it intended. Its memory is selective in a pattern nobody has mapped, including The Mop, which refers to itself in the third person without exception and has never explained why.

It is the only one who can see or hear Zack Fantana. The exceptions are three engrams sharing a frequency, and none of them are in the room when it matters.

The Mop thinks. The Mop knows. The Mop is aware.

The Job That Started It

Hired with Jackie Welles and the netrunner T-Bug by the fixer Dexter DeShaun to take a biochip out of DevlinSaka Tower. They saw something in that penthouse they were not supposed to see.

T-Bug went first, remotely. Jackie put the chip in his own neural port and died getting The Mop clear. Dexter shot The Mop and left it in a landfill. BBC settled that, then carried what was left to Max's clinic.

Asked honestly: The Mop would not have been altruistic with the championship either.
