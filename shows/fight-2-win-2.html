01

The Job

The heist went wrong the way heists go wrong — thoroughly, and to everyone. A biochip came out of DevlinSaka Tower. Three people went in for it. One walked out, and only because someone carried it.

What's on the chip is Zack Fantana, the world's leading Wrestling Symbologist, dead for fifty-one years and considerably more talkative about it than anyone expected. What's carrying him is The Mop, who does not remember agreeing to any of this and would like the record to reflect that.

Seven people vanished at WrestleMecca in 2026. Not killed — filed. Scattered into pocket dimensions and left there while the world moved on without noticing the gap. Max can find them. Max cannot bring them back alone. And the only thing that can undo what was done sits in a locked altar beneath the tallest building in a city that Brennan Devlin owns outright.

Everyone who wants the championship would use it. That's the problem, and everyone in the room knows it, and nobody says it out loud until it's far too late to matter.

02

The Seven Signals

Seven readings on Max's board. Seven places that shouldn't exist, each one built out of whatever the person inside it brought with them. All seven have been there seventy-seven years.

SIGNAL 01
Jove Belane
The Town · WrestleMecca Island
Kept a town alive against things wearing the faces of people he used to work with. Championship belts nailed above every door, because they hold. The serum is nearly gone. "I kept the lights on."
SIGNAL 02
JD Driftwood
Bone Mountain
Seventy-seven years and nothing to hit. Swung first, asked after. Stopped cold at the only fact that ever mattered to him: there was a title on the line and nobody let him compete for it. Left with a souvenir. The bone counts.
SIGNAL 03
Amy Stake
The House That Kept Building
A perfect home that never stopped adding rooms, and a competitor sealed inside it with nothing to beat. Finished serving the course before she left, because leaving early would have been rude. "Are you here to take me somewhere I can hit something?"
SIGNAL 04
Harper Lee Vale
The Island · West Side Cave
Survived the crash. Did not survive what she found in the wreckage, or what it spent fifty years telling her. The hardest retrieval in the film, and the only one where The Mop had to go somewhere first and ask permission.
SIGNAL 05
Hatchet Gully & A$hley Quidd
Utopia
Accidentally achieved world peace and cannot agree on how. Heard the gong every year for seventy-seven years, watched the planet lose its mind about it, and told nobody, because it was funny. Packed on a Tuesday. Had a curry first.
SIGNAL 06
Warrick Hill
The Continental United States
Not trapped anywhere exotic. Just never arriving. Seventy-seven years of connecting routes that don't connect, with a man whose map is wrong and a kid who fishes. Found on a broken swan paddleboat, six weeks into a slow circle.
SIGNAL 07
Waverly Winters
The Island · Jurassic
Four hundred and seventeen marks scratched into concrete. Surviving the fauna was the easy part. Reads a frequency Max has no category for — not the carrier wave, something next to it, and older.
SIGNAL 07·B
Ursula St. James
The Island · Same Coordinates
Fell through the same crack and landed in the same place, and has spent every day since trying to kill the other reading on this line. Has died approximately as many times as Waverly has counted. Her own count is five lower. She carries it on a bent piece of metal.
03

Five Readings, One Belt

Max has been tracking five signatures inside the same object for four decades. They predate human civilisation. She can measure them, chart them, and confirm they are getting stronger. She cannot tell you what they are. Her working name for whatever put them there is "Wrestling Space Gods," which nobody likes, including her.

Reading One
Force. Things move that should not move.
Reading Two
Endings. Whatever it closes stays closed.
Reading Three
Structure. It knows how the mechanism works.
Reading Four
Record. The history says what it says.
Reading Five
Unclassified. Max stopped writing notes on this one.

"That's the finish, BBC. That's the finish."

Zack Fantana · Wrestling Symbologist · 1994–2026
